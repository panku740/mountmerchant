NUTRITIONAL & FOOD ADDITIVES

Nutritional and food additives are functional ingredients used to enhance the quality, safety, stability, and nutritional value of food and beverage products. They play a crucial role in modern food manufacturing by improving texture, flavor, appearance, shelf life, and overall consumer acceptability. These additives include a broad range of substances such as emulsifiers, stabilizers, thickeners, antioxidants, preservatives, acidity regulators, and nutrient enhancers. In addition to supporting product performance, many additives also contribute to food fortification, helping address nutritional deficiencies and support public health initiatives. In the food and nutraceutical industries, additives are essential for developing innovative and consistent products that meet evolving consumer demands. With increasing focus on clean-label and natural ingredients, manufacturers are increasingly adopting additive solutions that deliver performance while aligning with transparency and sustainability goals

Cosmetics & Personal Care Creams, lotions, hair care, cleansing products Enhances texture, stabilizes formulations, improves spreadability and sensory feel
Pharmaceuticals Syrups, tablets, capsules, suspensions Supports drug stability, enhances taste masking, improves uniformity and shelf life
Animal Nutrition Feed premixes, supplements Improves feed stability, enhances nutrient delivery, supports animal growth and health

EMULSIFIERS

Additive Primary Role Application/Industry
PVPK-30 (Polyvinylpyrrolidone K-30) Stabilizer / Clarifier Beverage clarification (Beer/Wine), Binder in flavor/nutraceutical tablets, improving the solubility and delivery of actives.
PEG-6000 (Polyethylene Glycol 6000) Plasticizer / Thickener Texture enhancement (Ice Cream/Sauces), Anti-foaming agent in beverages, Lubricant for tablet pressing.
Fat Powder Creaminess / Mouthfeel Enhancer Instant coffee creamers, powdered nutritional shakes, and dry soup mixes, providing fat content in a stable, easy-to-mix dry format.
Soya Lecithin Emulsifier / Stabilizer Essential for preventing oil/water separation (Mayonnaise, Dressings), reducing chocolate viscosity for easier processing, and extending shelf life of baked goods.
Collagen Peptide Nutritional Fortifier / Texture Modifier Functional beverages and bars (for protein and joint health), acts as a non-gelling hydrocolloid to improve viscosity and body.
Aerosil (Silicon Dioxide) Anti-Caking Agent / Flow Agent Critical for powdered products (salt, spices, protein powders, coffee creamers) to absorb moisture, prevent clumping, and ensure smooth machine processing.
Maltodextrin Bulking Agent / Carrier / Texturizer Adding volume (Filler) to dry mixes, improving the mouthfeel (viscosity) of products.
