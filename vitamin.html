VITAMINS AND MINERALS

Vitamins and minerals are essential micronutrients that play a critical role in maintaining overall health, metabolic efficiency, and physiological balance. While required in small quantities, they are indispensable for energy production, immune support, bone health, cognitive function, and cellular protection. Vitamins support a wide range of biological processes, including metabolism, vision, skin health, and nervous system function, whereas minerals contribute to structural development, muscle contraction, fluid balance, and enzymatic activity. Together, they ensure optimal body performance and long-term wellness. Vitamins and minerals are widely used in dietary supplements, functional foods, and fortified beverages to address nutritional gaps and support targeted health needs. With increasing consumer awareness of preventive healthcare, high- quality, bioavailable, and stable micronutrient formulations have become essential components of modern nutrition solutions.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS

Moisture ≤ 5.0%
