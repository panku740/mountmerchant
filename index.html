Our Segments

PHARMACEUTICAL

We provide specialized pharmaceutical ingredients and excipients that support product safety, efficacy, and innovation. Our portfolio includes binders, disintegrants, stabilizers, coating agents, controlled-release systems, and fortification blends designed for tablets, capsules, syrups, and nutraceuticals. With a focus on precision and compliance, our solutions enhance bioavailability, improve formulation stability, and align with global regulatory standards.

NUTRACEUTICALS

We delivers premium nutraceutical raw materials engineered for high-performance health and wellness formulations. Our portfolio spans amino acids, vitamins, minerals, herbal extracts, proteins, enzymes, probiotics, and specialty actives. With a strong emphasis on purity, consistency, and dependable sourcing, we empower brands to create superior supplements, powders, capsules, and functional foods with confidence.

ANIMAL NUTRITION

We delivers premium raw materials that strengthen animal health, immunity, and performance. Our portfolio spans vitamin and mineral premixes, probiotics, enzymes, amino acids, and feed-grade additives for poultry, cattle, aquaculture, and pets. With a focus on quality, safety, and consistency, we enable manufacturers to produce dependable, high-nutrition feed formulations.

⁠DAIRY & FUNCTIONAL INGREDIENTS

We offers advanced dairy and functional ingredients that enhance nutrition, texture, flavour, and overall product efficiency. Our selection includes milk powders, whey proteins, caseinates, stabilizers, emulsifiers, probiotics, and fortification blends used across dairy, beverages, snacks, and wellness products. From yogurts to fortified foods, we provide high- quality solutions aligned with clean-label trends and improved consumer appeal.

AGRICULTURE

We connect producers and users of a variety of crops around the globe through origination, trading, processing, and distribution, as well as offering a range of farmer services and risk management solutions.

BAKERY & CONFECTIONERY

We supplies high-performance ingredients crafted for bakery and confectionery applications. The range covers stabilizers, emulsifiers, sweeteners, flavours, enzymes, cocoa derivatives, dairy powders, and texturizing agents that elevate taste, freshness, and uniformity. Ideal for breads, cakes, biscuits, chocolates, and candies, our solutions support superior quality and extended shelf life.

BEVERAGE

We deliver innovative beverage solutions designed to elevate taste, nutrition, and consumer satisfaction. Our portfolio includes advanced flavor systems, natural sweeteners, plant-based proteins, stabilizers, emulsifiers, and fortification blends tailored for a wide range of applications. From refreshing juices and energy drinks to functional waters and wellness beverages, we provide high-quality ingredients that support clean-label trends, improved texture, and enhanced nutritional value. With a focus on efficiency and consumer appeal, our beverage solutions help brands create products that stand out in today’s competitive market.

CEREALS

We offer advanced cereal ingredients and solutions that enhance nutrition, texture, and consumer appeal across breakfast and snack applications. Our portfolio includes whole grains, malt extracts, fiber blends, starches, fortification systems, and natural sweeteners designed to improve taste, crunch, and shelf stability.

BEAUTY & PERSONAL CARE

Empowering modern formulation excellence, Nutranova provides high-grade ingredients to the cosmetic and personal care industry. Our offerings include actives, botanicals, oils, emulsifiers, surfactants, vitamins, and functional additives suited for advanced skincare, haircare, and body care solutions. Built for superior performance, they improve texture, stability, and consumer appeal while supporting clean-label innovation.
